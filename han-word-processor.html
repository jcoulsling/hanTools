Typing Hän

You type in CLC romanisation and the syllabics appear as you go. Consonant clusters are typed exactly as they are written — th, dh, zh, sr, zr, tr, dr, tth, ddh, tl, dl, kh, gh, hw, khw, ch, ts, dz, sh.

Vowels, tone and nasals

Type the trigger key before the vowel. Triggers stack, so ,qi gives a nasal vowel carrying low tone.

Every vowel may be oral or nasal, and may carry tone.

ö is accepted anywhere and read as ë. A stray apostrophe becomes the glottal-stop final ᐧ. Switch dialect on the Hän tab — Eagle and Moosehide differ in how some finals are written.
